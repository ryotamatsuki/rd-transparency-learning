## Audited theorem and required exact game (not shorthand)

- A incumbent quality \(s\), B immediate but persistent quality \(b\), B learning-efficiency \(L=\lambda\); \(d=d_0+s-b\).
- First period B share \(D_1=(t-d+p_A-p_B)/(2t)\). Second period B relative quality \(q_2=-d+\lambda D_1\).
- Quadratic R&D costs and first-period Bertrand followed by second-period Bertrand.
- Exogenously given P: \(L\) observable to A before current pricing; H: no A-price response to B's **unobserved unilateral** change \(L\), though A rationally forecasts its equilibrium choice.
- **Repaired global theorem** requires \(s\in[0,\bar s], b\in[0,\bar b], L\in[0,\bar L]\), \(0<d_0-\bar b<d_0+\bar s<3t\), sufficiently small \(\bar L\), baseline interior quality solutions, stability \(k_s>c,k_b>c,\Delta>0\), high \(k_L\), extra incumbent-profit condition. Only unique **pure** equilibrium on these strategy sets is asserted. General unbounded claim FAILS a profitable corner/monopoly deviation. Normalized finite-cost result is a separate bounded theorem with its own threshold.
- Results: \(L^P=0<L^H\), \(s^P<s^H\), \(b^P>b^H\), \( \Pi_A^P>\Pi_A^H,\Pi_B^P>\Pi_B^H,W^P<W^H\). Benchmarked H-share lower in period 1, higher in period 2.
- Welfare reversal persists in a fixed-quality-investment counterfactual. Thus investment *composition* is **amplifier**, not **necessary causal driver**, of the private-social welfare sign inversion.

## Mathematical exact relation to general commitment theory (not new as a stand-alone theorem)

Let \(U_B\) denote B's continuation profit before its own price optimization. At the common no-learning baseline:
\[
\frac{\mathrm{d}V_B^P}{\mathrm{d}\lambda}-
\frac{\partial V_B^H}{\partial\lambda}
=\frac{\partial U_B}{\partial p_A}
  \frac{\partial p_A^*}{\partial\lambda}
=-\frac{\delta(3t-d)(9t+d)}{162t^2}<0
\]
for \(0<d<3t\). This is exactly the strategic-reaction/envelope term of the canonical models. Our own \(\lambda D_1\) technology determines the coefficient and the signs, but the derivative decomposition is **not an independent abstract theorem**. In the no-feedback control \(q_2=-d+\lambda\), the P/H differential effect vanishes in this information structure, but this shows **within-model identification**, not novelty of an information principle.

## The best short editor/referee objection to the present paper

> The paper combines established strategic investment commitment effects, experience-driven price competition and R&D observability in a two-period Hotelling specification. The direction of research reallocates within the chosen quadratic technology, but the striking profit–welfare reversal already appears in the differentiated Bertrand observability literature, and indeed persists in the present model with the quality-investment composition fixed. The general results require exogenous caps on quality and learning investments to exclude otherwise profitable monopoly-region deviations. The manuscript has not demonstrated an independent or sufficiently general theorem that would justify publication as a full field-journal theoretical article.

This is an **editorial assessment**, not a prediction of any particular editor's actual decision. A narrow specialist venue could reach a different assessment.
